AI role (keys A / B / C)

✓A Direct AI feedback

AI directly receives the student's submitted work (code, essay, exam response) and generates personalized feedback or a grade automatically — no human mediation before the student sees it.

✓B Human-mediated AI feedback

AI generates feedback or a grade based on student work, but a human instructor reviews, edits, or approves the output before the student receives it.

✓C Student-discretion AI use

Students query a general-purpose AI tool at their own discretion. The AI does not systematically receive or respond to specific student submissions; students choose when and how to use it.
